# Privacy Policy — Vazhe (واژه)

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_ · [فارسی](PRIVACY_POLICY.fa.md)

This Privacy Policy applies to **Vazhe (واژه)**, an offline English⇄Persian dictionary app for Android (package `app.vazhe.dictionary`) and its offline web version, published by **GiTi Tech** ("we", "us", "our"). You can reach us at **jghulami2000@gmail.com**.

## 1. Summary

- **We do not collect, receive, store, sell, share or analyze any personal data about you.**
- The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking and no third-party data-collecting SDKs.
- The Android app does not request the `INTERNET` permission and makes no network requests. The whole dictionary is inside the app.
- Your history, favorites, wordbook and settings stay **on your device only**. We cannot see them.
- You can delete everything yourself at any time (see section 8).

## 2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or ask for: your name, email address, phone number, address, account or login details, payment information, precise or approximate location, contacts, calendar, photos, files, camera or microphone data, advertising ID, device identifiers (such as IMEI or Android ID), IP address, cookies, usage analytics, search queries, or any other personal information. The app does not request any Android permission to access these things.

## 3. Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following **only inside the app's private storage on your device**:

- your settings (interface language, theme, text size, speech rate),
- your history of viewed words and your favorite words,
- your personal wordbook, your category names and your flashcard progress (Leitner box and due dates).

This data is created and used locally at your request to provide the features you use. It is never uploaded, synced or sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot access, recover or restore it. The app opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so this data is not copied to cloud backups by the app.

## 4. Features that involve other software on your device

- **Pronunciation (text-to-speech).** When you tap the speaker button, the app hands the English word to the text-to-speech engine you have selected in your Android settings (for example Google, Samsung or another vendor). That engine is a separate system component, not part of Vazhe, and is governed by its vendor's privacy policy. Depending on the engine and voice you choose, the vendor's software may process text in its own way; Vazhe itself sends nothing over the network.
- **Backup export and import.** The wordbook export creates a JSON file **only when you tap the button**. The file contains the words, categories, favorites and flashcard progress you saved — not your identity. It is passed to the Android share sheet, where **you** choose where it goes (for example your files, a cloud drive or a messaging app). Once you send it to another app or service, that app's or service's privacy policy applies. The exported file is not encrypted; treat it like any personal file. Import only reads a file you choose; it is not uploaded anywhere.
- **Links that open outside the app.** Some screens contain links (for example to this policy, license texts, Wiktionary, Tatoeba). Tapping one opens your browser, and the website you visit may receive technical information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. Vazhe does not contact these sites by itself.
- **Android System WebView.** The app draws its screens with the Android System WebView component supplied with your device. It is part of the operating system and is not used by Vazhe to send data anywhere.
- **Google Play.** If you install the app from Google Play, Google processes information about your download and device under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive personal data from Google. Google may give us aggregated, non-identifying statistics about the app (for example installs by country, Android version, or crash and performance reports from users who chose to share diagnostics with Google); these do not come from code inside Vazhe and do not identify you.
- **Web version.** If you use the offline web version in a browser, it stores the same local data in your browser's storage and caches the dictionary files in your browser. The server that hosts the web page may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) under its own policy; we do not receive or analyze those logs and do not use analytics or cookies.
- **This policy page.** This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data about visitors to this page under GitHub's privacy statement; we do not receive that data.

## 5. Sharing, selling and advertising

We do not sell, rent, trade, disclose or share personal information. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA). We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or training AI models, and we do not collect any sensitive personal information. In the 12 months before the date above we collected, sold and shared no categories of personal information.

## 6. Legal basis and our role

Because we do not collect or receive personal data, we do not act as a controller or processor of personal data about you in the sense of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR or similar laws. Data that stays on your device is created and controlled by you.

## 7. Children

Vazhe is a general-audience dictionary and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the app collects none. The app is not part of Google Play's "Designed for Families" program. A dictionary can contain words that some people find offensive. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your choices, access and deletion

- **Delete in the app:** Settings → **Delete all my data** removes history, favorites, wordbook, categories, flashcard progress and settings. You can also clear only history (Saved → History → Clear) or remove single words and categories.
- **Delete from your device:** Android Settings → Apps → Vazhe → Storage → **Clear data**, or **uninstall** the app.
- **Web version:** clear the site's data in your browser settings.
- **Backup files** you exported are ordinary files you control; delete them wherever you saved or sent them.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California and other US states, Canada, Brazil, Australia and other countries), you may have rights over your personal data, such as to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, move your data, withdraw consent, opt out of sale or sharing, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or transfer, and you already control all locally stored data directly in the app (section 8). We never discriminate against anyone for exercising privacy rights.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to answer you, keep them no longer than needed, and do not share them.

## 10. Security

We reduce risk by design: no accounts, no servers and no data transmission mean there is no database of user data to breach. Local data is protected by the Android app sandbox and your device's own security (screen lock and storage encryption); we recommend keeping them enabled. No method is perfectly secure, and exported backup files are your responsibility.

## 11. International users

Because we do not collect personal data, no personal data is transferred across borders by us.

## 12. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

The app does not track you, so it does not need to react to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals.

## 13. Open-source components and data

The app code is MIT-licensed. The dictionary data comes from open sources (Wiktionary via kaikki.org, Tatoeba, Princeton WordNet) and the Vazirmatn font; these are bundled inside the app and never fetched at run time. Attributions and licenses are in the app (Settings → About & licenses) and in `NOTICE.md`. The bundled libraries (Capacitor and its filesystem, share and text-to-speech plugins) run entirely on your device and do not collect data.

If a change would mean collecting personal data, we will update the policy, the Google Play Data safety form and the in-app text before shipping it.
